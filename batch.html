The pre-campaign tool's flux formula is the primary decision: it doesn't need or assume a tunable batch size. This calculator is for the subset of test architectures that do support a configurable checkpoint interval — it refines throughput at a flux you've already chosen. If your test's work cycle is fixed by the application, this page has nothing you need; the flux formula alone is enough.

Model inputs

Measure \(W_0\) and \(t_s\) on the bench from event-free work cycles. Measure \(\lambda\) from a short pilot at the beam. The formula applies to any device with a checkpoint-restart work cycle, not only the MSP430.

ms

Per-cycle cost: compute setup + telemetry overhead, measured from event-free iterations.

ms per result

Per-result cost within a batch: compute + telemetry for each additional result.

s⁻¹

Work-interrupting triggers per beam-burst second. Measure at the target flux.

s

Mean recovery cost per interruption. Does not move B* (scales absolute time only). Default: 0.165 s protocol-terminal proxy.

Per-configuration presets

Set λ from a measured configuration. W₀ and tₛ stay at the values above.

Computed operating point

Curve: normalized throughput \(B\lambda/\mathrm{expm1}[\lambda W(B)]\). The optimum is flat-bottomed; the penalty for missing \(B^*\) by a factor of two is less than 20%.
