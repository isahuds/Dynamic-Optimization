Heterogeneity

Exposure-normalized FRAM builds are not statistically homogeneous under Tier 1+2 (p=0.0001). This is a real finding, not a classification artifact (checked and ruled out). Part of the SRAM/FRAM contrast is a confirmed, source-grounded storage-buffer persistence mechanism. Part is an honest, unresolved residual specific to FRAM_B1.

The buffer-persistence mechanism: FRAM ECC protects stored data in the buffer, but not the CPU and MPY32 computation path. An ion strike during computation can corrupt a result that is then stored correctly by ECC. The B/BG (buffer vs. background) attribution tracks the storage medium, not the batch size. This is why the SRAM configuration, which lacks ECC protection on the storage path, shows a higher operational rate.

Flux relationship

The derived operational cross section scales as \(\sigma\propto\phi^{-0.96}\), consistent with a paralyzable pileup mechanism rather than simple saturation. The fitted pileup model (\(R^2=0.386\)) substantially outperforms flat-saturation (\(R^2=0.11\)) and power-law (\(R^2=0.004\)) alternatives.
