What this page does

A work cycle survives when it finishes without a functional interrupt. Its chance of doing so depends on the flux, the length of the cycle, and one cross section for losing a cycle, \(\sigma_{\mathrm{cyc}}\). A target share of surviving cycles \(\theta\) therefore fixes the product of flux and work-cycle time. Read it one way and it is the flux to run for the work cycle you have; read it the other way and it is the longest work cycle a flux you have already chosen allows.

The survival form is the one used for checkpointed computation (Young 1974; Daly 2006). Choosing flux from it is the method of Zimmaro et al. (RADECS 2022), which this site's companion paper tests against a direct count of surviving cycles on heavy ions and extends. This page charges nothing for recovery time; the recovery page does.

The share of attempted work cycles you want to survive. The choice is yours. The companion paper illustrates with 0.90; the published method sets \(\theta\) indirectly by maximizing the chance of seeing a rarer failure first, and other guidance suggests about ten cycles between two losses of function.

The rule

$$\theta = e^{-\sigma_{\mathrm{cyc}}\,\phi\,W} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \phi\,W = \frac{-\ln\theta}{\sigma_{\mathrm{cyc}}}$$

Every pair of flux and work-cycle time with the same product delivers the same share of surviving cycles, so a tenfold higher flux is paid for by a tenfold shorter work cycle. \(\phi W\) is the fluence one cycle receives.

How the target trades against time

Loosening \(\theta\) raises the flux the rule allows and shortens the run. Tightening it does the reverse. Each row uses the inputs above.

Which cross section to use

Only a cross section counted from lost cycles over in-cycle fluence belongs in the rule. A functional-interrupt cross section from a reset counter, or one from the host's recovery record, counts over the full beam-on fluence, which credits exposure during windows in which no cycle could be interrupted. Either will predict more surviving cycles than a test delivers, by an amount that grows with how much time the device spends outside its work cycle. In the companion campaign the host's record gave a rate about half the fitted one. See the evidence.
